# Tollbound: the spine, composed for depth

Priority: depth for the builder. One principle: **each kind of harm asks one question of the body it lands on** (its weight, its muffling, its depth), and the tide, the crowd's shape and the haul change the answers. Every protection opens another harm. The frame is kept as given.

## 2. The combat model

### 2.1 Kinds of harm

| Kind | Asks | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Stroke** | weight | Lands at once with a weight 1 to 5 (the sigil's or the attacker's). On a body of its own weight it is **true**: full harm, and 50% of it again as Ring. On a lighter body it **breaks**: full harm, and 25% again as Crack. On a heavier body it **taps**: half harm. |
| **Ring** | muffling | Sounds in the body evenly over 3 s. Starting or renewed, it passes 30% of the new Ring to up to 8 bodies of the same weight within 3 m; passed Ring passes no further. On a muffled body it lands at once as Crack at half. |
| **Press** | depth | Lands at once, times the band at the victim's feet: dry 0.5, ankle 1, knee 1.5, deep 2. Weight 5 bodies count a band deeper, weight 1 a band shallower. Each Press on a body in water adds 1 Brine. |
| **Crack** | nothing | Takes life and greatest life together. Each further quarter of its starting greatest life cracked drops a body one weight class (never keepers or the player). Comes from breaks, Ring on the muffled, drying Brine and converting bindings. |

A sigil deals one kind or several; bindings convert ("gain 20% of Stroke as extra Ring"). Size shows a dead body's weight, from 1, the small dead, to 5, the bell-dead. Defender order: weight match, Press band, Ring or its Crack, Crack less Anneal, harm-taken modifiers, the player's wound.

### 2.2 Protections

| Against | Protection | Effect | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stroke | **Weight** | Class 1 below 50 Weight, 2 from 50, 3 from 100, 4 from 175, 5 from 275; characters start at 50. Lighter strokes tap. | **Heft**: each class above 2 adds 0.08 s to reaching full pace, stopping and reversing; class 5 sits a band deeper. |
| Ring | **Muffle** | No Ring sounds in the character. | It lands as Crack at half instead. |
| Press | **Freeboard** | Each point counts water a band shallower, two at most. | Each point carries the character 25% farther on currents and shoves. |
| Crack | **Anneal** | Takes its share off Crack, 60% at most. | Its main source, gun-metal, means little Tin. |

A body is **Muffled** while knee-deep or deeper, while three other bodies touch it, or while wrapped in cloth by a sigil, casting or vow. Weight comes from Copper, castings and haul. Heavy blocks blows and sinks; muffled stops Ring and cracks; floating escapes Press and drifts.

### 2.3 The tide

Each toll turns the tide, odd tolls to flood and even to ebb. Through the 75 s the level moves by 1, 2, 3, 3, 2, 1 twelfths of its range in steps of 12.5 s, slack for 3 s either side of the toll. Every fourth toll is a spring of double range; the eighth, the keeper's, is the highest water. Depth is level less ground height; the current runs along each district's channels as fast as the level moves. Ring peaks on the ebb, Press on the flood, and Brine lands on the turn.

### 2.4 Conditions

1. **Ringing.** From Ring: 12 even ticks over 3 s; new Ring adds to the rest, restarts the 3 s and spreads. Ends after 3 s, or on becoming Muffled, the rest landing as Crack at half.
2. **Muffled.** While knee water, three touching bodies or a wrapping lasts: no Ringing, none passed on; Ring lands as Crack at half.
3. **Cracked.** From Crack: greatest life lowered and a class lost per quarter, so lighter blows begin to ring true on it. Enemies keep it; the player keeps it to the vigil's end, but standing within 6 m of the bell-post at a toll recasts a quarter.
4. **Swing.** Only alternation builds it: a Stroke arriving more than 120° off the previous Stroke's direction adds 1, one within 60° removes 1; it never fades. At 6 (keepers 12) the body goes over: thrown 4 m (keepers 1 m) along the last stroke, every body within 1 m of the path taking a Stroke of its weight worth 10% of its greatest life. The player at 6 is only shoved 2 m.
5. **Brined.** From Press in water, a stack each, 20 at most, inert in water. On dry ground every stack lands as Crack worth 4% of greatest life (elites 2%, keepers 1%, the player 1.5%), then no stacks for 3 s. A second in fresh water (fonts, rain) rinses all.
6. **In Procession.** From following 5 s unbroken within 2.5 m of the player's path of the last 4 s: +15% speed, +25% harm taken, +25% experience, never crowd-muffled, counted by modifiers up to 40. Ends on touching the player, a shove, or straying 3 m. A loose line of like bodies is Ring's best ground.
7. **Under Seal.** From wax. No condition expires, starts or ends, muffling included: a sealed body rings on in a crowd or water, and its Brine cannot land. Ends on one hit of 8% of greatest life, or after 15 s. On the player it holds boons and harms alike.
8. **Sounded.** From holding a weight 4 or 5 body or a keeper 4 to 6 m away for 2 s. For 6 s, renewed while held, every Stroke on it lands true whatever its weight, and its attacks show 0.3 s earlier.

### 2.5 How the player heals

Every Stroke, Ring or Press the player takes is chalked as a **wound** where it landed; Ring joins its stroke's wound, and wounds within 1 m join. Standing still within 1 m of a wound for its term (how long its blow was shown, at least 0.5 s) mends it. At most 12 lie open; a thirteenth hardens the oldest into Crack. Crack mends only at the bell-post at a toll; between vigils a character is whole. The common dead aim at the player's place of 1.2 s ago, so a long mend means leading the train off and looping back.

### 2.6 The two buttons

**Trade** is the class's tool (Section 4). **Heave** throws the heaviest find carried 5 m ahead as a Stroke of the thrower's weight, harm set by the find's worth (recharge 4 s). Each find carried adds 3 Weight and takes 1.5% off pace. A heaved find drifts with the current until caught, or lies on dry ground.

### 2.7 Enemies and the player

One rule set: a ringing player spreads Ring to the dead of its weight, and a crowd touching it muffles it. Beyond what 2.1 to 2.5 state, the dead's weights are fixed while the player's moves; the common dead steer at the player's place of 1.2 s ago, elites and keepers at the player; enemies never mend; only enemies are Sounded or In Procession.

### 2.8 What the screen shows

- Size is weight; sigil icons carry weight notches; the player's class sits by its life bar.
- A tap is a dull puff, a true stroke a bright ripple, a break grey chips; passed Ring is an arc to each body reached.
- Ringing pulses rings; Muffled wears a cloth band or water at the knee; Cracked shows a split and steps down a size; Swing a tick arc from 3; Brined white crust; Under Seal a red wax disc; Sounded two thin rings; a procession a faint thread along the path, with a count.
- Water as itself, tide marks on pillars, the foot ring coloured by band; wounds as chalk rings ticked per half second of term.
- One dial for toll and tide, marking the next gate, spring and keeper; the candle beside it.
- The toll counts what comes: nine strokes for mostly weight 4 and 5 in the next 75 s, six for 2 and 3, three for 1; a quick stroke per elite; a low one if a gate will open (or marks, without sound).

### 2.9 What builds hook onto

Every number above is a stat that the Plan, bindings, castings and chimes move, under any of the model's conditions (in flood, at slack, in deep water, against the cracked). Light sigils clear loose crowds of their weight on dry ground; heavy ones never tap and never spread; water builds load on the flood and cash in on the ebb. Each is strong for part of every 150 s.

### 2.10 The model in four sentences

Every sigil and every body has a weight from 1 to 5: a stroke of the body's own weight rings true, hurting it again over three seconds and passing some of that to like bodies nearby; a heavier stroke cracks it, taking greatest life that does not come back; a lighter one only taps for half. Water at the knee, a cloth wrapping or a tight crowd muffles a body, so it cracks instead of ringing. Press, the harm of water, grows with the depth at the victim's feet and leaves salt that cracks the body when it reaches dry ground, and the tide turns at every toll, so the best ground for each harm keeps moving. Your wounds stay where they landed: stand still on one for as long as its blow was shown before it fell, and it mends.

## 3. Attributes

- **Copper**: +5 greatest life, +1 Weight per point.
- **Tin**: +2% Stroke harm, +1% Ring length per point.
- **Rope**: +1% to how often sigils speak, +1% to their reach per point.
- **Metal**, read from Tin divided by Copper plus Tin, on a gauge: under 15% **gun-metal**, +40% Anneal; 15% to 25% **bell-metal**, the character's Ring spreads 50% farther and to 2 more bodies; over 25% **speculum**, +25% Stroke and +40% Crack taken.

Each level a class gains Copper/Tin/Rope in its mix: Ringer 3/1/1, Tuner 2/1/2, Leadsman 3/0/2, Warper 4/0/1, Sexton 4/1/0, Chandler 1/1/3. Plan lanes, lines and chimes add more.

**How things ask.** Everything equipped is cast in one metal or in any. A sigil of another metal than the character's speaks at 75%; a casting or binding of another metal works at half; a casting cast in a metal rolls one step truer than one cast in any. One point of Tin can carry the gauge across a mark and change half the kit, so points are planned against the kit and the kit against the points.

## 4. Classes and callings

**Ringer**, a tower ringer who kept hold of the rope. *Thumb:* rocks back and forth in the crowd. *Sigil:* Tenor Stroke, weight 4, everything within 2.5 m every 3 s. *Rule, the pull:* a reversal of more than 120° makes every Stroke sigil at least half ready speak at once (each at most once per 0.4 s); lifting the thumb 1 s sets the bell, doubling the next pull. Rocking past bodies strikes them from alternate sides, building Swing. *Trade, Set:* sets the bell while walking at half pace.
- **Stander:** while set, each body within 2 m leans on the stay and adds its weight to a shown store (limit 15); the next step releases every Stroke sigil at +10% per point leaning. Past 15 the stay breaks: store lost, no sigil for 3 s.
- **Half-muffler:** pulls alternate handstroke and backstroke, a handstroke after each set; a backstroke deals half and cannot ring but wraps what it strikes for 2 s, so the next handstroke cracks it.

**Tuner**, a foundry tuner who cut bells to their note. *Thumb:* how far it is pushed. *Sigil:* Fork, a ring at the note's weight: 5, 6 m every 2.4 s; 4, 5 m every 1.8 s; 3, 4 m every 1.3 s; 2, 3 m every 0.9 s; 1, 2 m every 0.6 s. *Rule, pace is pitch:* a push under 20% (or standing) is weight 5, each further 20% one lighter, and every Stroke the Tuner makes takes that weight. *Trade, Hold:* keeps the note 5 s whatever the pace (recharge 12 s).
- **Gallery-walker:** a sigil cast within 2 m of a wall runs along it both ways 20 m at 10 m/s, round corners, striking every body within 1.5 m of the wall once, instead of spreading.
- **Node-setter:** every 12 s a fork is planted, holding that note for 12 s. On the line from fork to Node-setter (to 14 m), every 2 m a loud point, a 1 m circle, strikes twice a second at that weight; halfway between, still points where no Ring harms anyone.

**Leadsman**, who sounds the flooded streets ahead of the barges. *Thumb:* holds everything at 5 m, circling and stepping back. *Sigil:* Lead Line, a lead circling at 5 m every 1.5 s that strikes only bodies 4.3 to 5.7 m away, a weight 3 Stroke and an equal Press, times the bodies in the band over those inside it (at least 1), up to 4 times. *Rule, sounding:* any body 1 s in the band is Sounded, whatever its weight. *Trade, Cast the lead:* Sounds the nearest weight 4 or 5 body within 10 m at once (recharge 10 s).
- **Deep-caller:** standing still 1 s resets the band to the nearest body's distance, 2 to 8 m, until the next such stop.
- **Pilot:** under way within 1.5 m of the line through the bell-post and the last gate opened, the band widens 0.5 m a second (to +3 m) and sigils reach 10% farther a second (to +50%); stopping or straying loses both.

**Warper**, who moves hulks through the flood by anchor and line. *Thumb:* points; it never walks. *Sigil:* Kedge, each landing a weight 4 Stroke within 1.2 m. *Rule, warping:* every 0.9 s a kedge flies 4.5 m where the thumb points (6 m in knee water) and the Warper is hauled to it in 0.35 s, unpushable, the taut line dealing Press to what it crosses; thumb lifted, it stands. *Trade, Belay:* makes fast, immovable, the last line fixed and dealing Press; press again to cast off.
- **Snubber:** a line bent round a post swings the Warper round it, sweeping, to land beyond; a full turn snubs: 3 s immovable, shoving back whatever pushes.
- **Wrecker:** Belay instead lights the last kedge for 6 s. Bodies within 12 m with a clear line walk straight at it, unable to turn, gaining 1 m/s a second; whatever they run onto deals them a Stroke of their weight worth 5% of greatest life per m/s.

**Sexton**, who digs the parish graves and tolls the passing bell. *Thumb:* lifts to dig, then judges when to climb out. *Sigil:* Spadeful, every 1.2 s a weight 2 Stroke on the nearest body within 5 m, wrapping it 2 s. *Rule, the grave:* standing still digs a place a second, up to 8. In it the Sexton is Muffled and reached only by shown strikes. A body walking in is laid with full experience, filling as many places as its weight; elites and keepers stop at the lip. Moving climbs out at 0.5 s a place, open to blows; a full grave closes. *Trade, Close:* shuts the grave, out in 0.3 s, the mound wrapping what stands on it 4 s.
- **Fossor:** each grave throws a 3 m spoil bank opposite the Fossor's approach; the dead cannot cross it and are turned round its ends toward the pit while the grave is open.
- **Sin-eater:** 0.5 s on the spot where a body fell, within 3 s, eats its sin: double experience and 1 Sin (+1% harm, -0.5% pace, no limit), shed only at a gate, a point to an Echo.

**Chandler**, the candle-maker with a rod of hanging wicks. *Thumb:* runs loops out 12 m and back to the vat. *Sigil:* Broach, five weight 1 wicks lashing 3 m ahead every 1.5 s; three of one lash on a body put it Under Seal. *Rule, dipping:* each return within 2 m of the vat after going 12 m away adds a coat, up to 8: +6% harm each; a hit strips one and is halved. Coated, the Chandler is waxed: no Brine gathers, and Press on it is +25%. *Trade:* moves the vat to its feet (recharge 20 s).
- **Founder:** at 8 coats, a return with Trade held pours them into one Bronze for the vigil (+12% harm, +15 Weight, never stripped); coats restart from none.
- **Tenebrist:** at each toll it darkens one of its own sigils, vows or chimes for the vigil, and an heir of the same kind takes on all of it: a sigil heir the darkened one's harm per second at that toll, a vow heir its effect (its inches freed), a chime heir its effect.

They move and decide differently: rocking in place, choosing pace, holding a distance, hopping, standing still, looping to a base.

## 5. The world

**Pitch.** Tollbound is a cathedral-city on a tidal river. Its founders cast great bells to keep the drowned and the buried asleep, and rang them at every turn of the tide. Now the bells are silent. In each of the five districts the great bell still swings at the turn but makes no sound, and every silent stroke wakes more dead. You are one of the last living ringers, sent from the Loft, the one dry tower, to keep vigil in one district at a time. Lay the dead, carry your finds out through the water-gates, and when the keeper comes down at the eighth stroke, beat it so the bell speaks again.

**Tone.** (1) Working people, working dead: no chosen ones, no villain, no gore. (2) Every effect has a cause you could build: metal cracks, water damps, wax seals, salt dries; nothing glows unless it burns. (3) Silence is the danger: a well city rings, and harm shows as stillness and rising water.

**Naming.** Plain English from bell-founding, river work, burial custom and candle-making; no invented syllables or Latin. Districts are "The" and a feature (The Founders' Sink); each bell has a short name (the Unpoured Bell) and its keeper is "Keeper of" it, except the last. The dead are named for what they were or how they were buried (Bell-bearers, Salter), sigils for a trade's verb or tool (Spadeful), castings for object and maker (Lead Pattens). Characters read "Agnes, Sexton, 212 tolls".

**The districts, in order.**

1. **The Founders' Sink.** Tapered-box furnaces, cylinder chimneys, cone slag, round casting pits; soot black, loam brown, ember orange, bronze, verdigris. Dry but for knee-deep channels: Ring's ground. A Stroke landing in a pit is answered 1 s later by a 4 m Ring of the same harm on all alike; a pit answered over 3 times in 5 s cracks for the vigil. Bell: the Unpoured Bell. **Keeper of the Unpoured Bell:** (1) a wax model whose eight faces record the harm each takes, dripping wax circles (1 s) that set into pillars; (2) at half life bronze takes its exact place, the most harmed faces taking double and untouched ones a fifth; it turns its thin faces away at 30° a second, swinging a 4 m arc shown 1 s ahead.
2. **The Hanging Quarter.** Oak box frames, lead floors, striped cylinder sallies; oak brown, lead grey, wool red, chalk white, woad blue. Forty ropes tie bells overhead to circles 5 to 10 m away: walking through a sally drops a weight 5 Stroke on its circle 1 s later, and the dead blunder through. Bell: the Dead Band. **Conductor of the Dead Band:** six bells each tilt 0.6 s before a 40° wedge of Ring, in a row shown by numbered lamps: (1) rounds, 1 to 6; (2) at 70%, changes, each bell keeping its place or swapping with a neighbour, so each row reads from the last; (3) at 30%, all six after 2 s, except bells struck to nothing, which leave safe wedges.
3. **The Nave.** Two rows of cylinder pillars, box pews adrift; drowned green, slate, candle gold, lichen white, soot. Over the tide the water sloshes end to end every 24 s, one end deep while the other is at the ankle; the middle never changes depth but runs fastest. Pews ride it and strike at weight 4; fonts rinse Brine. Bell: the Sacring Bell. **Keeper of the Hearse,** 15 candles each lighting a 24° sector: (1) a candle flares 1.2 s and its sector is swept and goes dark; touching the frame within 2 s relights it and wounds the keeper; (2) at 5 candles, two at once; (3) the last hides for 5 s of dark and three claps, each 0.8 s ahead, then the keeper takes triple harm for 6 s.
4. **The Strand.** Stepped-box stairs, cylinder posts, three tapered-box hulks; mud brown, tin-grey water, bone, weed green, buoy red. Each ebb bares 30 m of silt that slows all but wearers of pattens and shows buried finds; each flood floats the hulks to ground elsewhere. The stairs stay dry, and Brine lands there. Bell: the Sunk Bell. **Keeper of the Sunk Bell:** (1) unharmable outside its great bell over deep water; every 20 s the bell's shadow falls on the player 1.5 s, then it drops, shutting all within 5 m in with the keeper for 10 s on dry ground, a candle measuring the air (when it dies, harm each second; the player may snuff it to save air and fight blind); (2) two candles, half the air.
5. **The Close.** Flagstones, crosses of two boxes on steps, a tapered-box west front; limestone, lead grey, verdigris, candle gold, window blue. Rings round the cathedral door at 30, 15 and 4 m cut the dead's harm by 20%, 40% and 70%, for 40 s of each ring per character per vigil. Bell: the Sleeper. **The Flogged Bell,** the Sleeper carried by the dead: (1) tongueless, it charges along lines shown 1 s ahead, shedding four dead at each wall; (2) at 60%, re-tongued, it tolls every 6 s after a 1.5 s swell, filling the arena with Ring; shelter is the sound-shadow 3 m behind a weight 5 body, or knee water where the Ring cracks at half; (3) at 25% it tells the player's years, a 2 m strike per level shown 0.8 s ahead, then lies spent 8 s.

**The frame's fiction.** The **Loft**, the ringing chamber of the one tower above high water, holds the foundry, the stash and the **Carillon**, where founders recast each fallen ringer as a bell weighing the tolls it heard; a fallen ringer is never brought back (**one life**). Every toll a living ringer hears leaves **Echoes** in the stone at once. Each district's bell swings silent at every turn of the tide (**the toll**), waking more dead and raising rewards; the eighth brings the keeper, whose fall gives the bell its voice and opens the district's charts. **Gates** are water-gates open 25 s at the slack of every third toll and where an elite or keeper falls; standing in one rings down six strokes a second apart, each banking a sixth of the haul in a set order and silencing a sixth of the sigils, the sixth ending the vigil; a ringer driven off sooner keeps what was banked and fights on. When all five bells speak, the districts join along their tide-ways into one open city.

## 6. Progression

### The Plan: the shared tree

A street plan of Tollbound, about 700 nodes, cut by the river, each class's door on its edge: **lanes** (attributes, small stats), **corners** (a stat with a tag or condition), **squares** (a rule). A point per level to 70, plus deeds: about 85.

- **One route:** points lay one unbranching walk from the door, each moving the head to a neighbour; stepping onto a held node costs a point and doubles it, once; points come back only from the head, for coin.
- **Beaten bounds:** closing a loop grants every node inside, up to 12, at half strength, free.
- **Banks:** wet-bank nodes work only in water ankle-deep or more, dry-bank nodes only on dry ground, both at 160%; streets and bridges always at 100%.
- **Straight miles:** milestones gain 25% per held node beyond the fourth in their straight street, up to 10.

Planning is drawing with three strokes (straights for miles, loops for bounds, doubling back for doubles), weighing fenced ground against fence, in an order that matters because only the head comes back. The bank decides whether the build peaks on the flood, the ebb or neither: the tide's question to the thumb, asked again.

### Castings: gear

A casting has a slot (tool, crown, yoke, gloves, girdle, pattens, two tokens), a Weight added to the wearer's, a metal or none, and up to five **lines**, hum to nominal. A line is a clause (verb: stat and amount; object: tags; condition: when), such as I CRACK / THE HEAVY / IN DEEP WATER, drawn from every rule of Section 2. Each rolls 0 to 6 steps sharp of its true amount and works at 100% less 10% a step, or 200% exactly true; later tolls drop truer finds; one line in ten is mis-stamped, its condition reversed. No tiers: a casting is worth its distance from true and the Weight left to get there.

Changes at the Loft's foundry are one-way:
- **Lathe:** a cut moves a line a step toward true for 5% of the casting's Weight; past true it works at 50%; under 70% of its cast Weight every line halves.
- **Chisel:** strikes a line's object or condition, widening it to everything, or always, at 60%.
- **Recast:** pours two or three castings of a slot into one, Weight their sum less 10%; the first poured gives the low lines and the last the high (two: 1 to 3, then 4 and 5; three: 1 and 2, 3, then 4 and 5). Each toll the account hears before it is first worn moves its sharpest line a step toward true.

Truing spends Weight, which is protection; recasting buys it back with the parents.

### Chimes, vows, callings, charts

- **Chimes:** six stand in a visible row. At a level-up places 1 to 3 rise as shapes 4 m away and walking into one takes it; untaken, they sink after 8 s and the next offer raises 1 to 4. A new chime enters at 6, and after each level-up neighbours swap, alternately 1-2, 3-4, 5-6 and 2-3, 4-5, so what is coming shows. Examples: the Lathe's Cut (a sigil a weight lighter; a fourth cut, below 1, taps everything), Drill the Crack (today's Crack locked in, no more taken), Turn of the Tide, Tide-marked.
- **Vows:** the vigil candle is 12 inches; a free inch burns at each toll; a vow holds 2 to 5 inches unburnt; with none free, every vow goes dark until a keeper drops 3 inches of wax. Examples: Hand on the Bell (wrapped: no Ring, and blows of its weight crack), Float-light (a band shallower per quarter of life missing), Rounds (sigils speak in rows, lightest first, harder on bodies the row has struck).
- **Callings and rites:** the calling is chosen at the first keeper beaten: its rule and 8 points of nodes. A rite is a vigil keeping one custom from first toll to keeper (at most three finds; no wound hardened), worth 2 calling points.
- **Charts and deeds:** keepers of voiced districts drop charts, a district with up to three marks (every toll a spring; the dead a weight heavier), each raising danger and Echoes. Deeds are stated acts (lay a keeper with Ring alone) granting classes, pits or castings.

### The Carillon: the permanent tree

- **Echoes:** the Nth toll of a vigil pays N times the number of different landmarks the character has stood beside (within 4 m) at a toll this vigil. Every vigil pays, fatal or not, and each toll asks for a new place.
- **The frame** branches from the tenor pit; Echoes open a pit beside an open one, each with a printed grant (flat, inc or parameter only: an inch of candle, a seventh chime place).
- **The dead:** each dead or retired character becomes a bell weighing the tolls it heard in life, hung by weight in an open pit. It grants every later character a boon fixed by its class and calling (Echoes can recast it) and names its killer: later characters deal 1% more to that kind per 5 levels it had.
- **True ring:** each neighbouring pair whose weights differ by 10% to 30% adds a step to the ring bonus (1% harm, 2% Echoes), so a ringer who died young fills a place a veteran cannot.

### The Wake

Held for one dead standing character (before any death, an unknown ringer). The new character starts at level 1 in its class with its first three chimes taken and builds itself through five districts, a watch each; keep all five and the dead one's bell counts double. Each Wake's order is the last with one neighbouring pair swapped by the player; an order never rung pays 25% more Echoes.
