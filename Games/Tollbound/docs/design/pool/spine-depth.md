# Tollbound: the spine, composed for depth

Priority: depth for the builder. **Each kind of harm asks one question of the body it lands on**: its weight, its muffling, its depth. Tide, crowd and haul change the answers, and every protection opens another harm. The frame is kept.

## 1. The game's words

- Active skill, **sigil**: a founder's device cast on a bell.
- Socketed modifier, **binding**, in 2 to 5 **loops**: how a clapper is bound sets its stroke.
- Reserved effect, **vow**; its pool, **the vigil candle**, in inches: a vow holds candle back from the flame.
- In-run upgrade, **chime**: a small bell hung for one night.
- Subclass, **calling**: a trade's higher office; a conductor's call.
- Calling challenge, **rite**: a duty to the dead, done exactly.
- Endgame map item, **chart**: a pilot's sounding of a drowned district.
- Quest-like goal, **deed**: an act in the parish book.
- Permanent tree, **the Carillon**; currency, **Echoes**: the Loft's frame of bells; what tolls leave in its stone.
- Ways to play, **the Watch** (standing characters) and **the Wake** (one sitting): a nightly watch; a night held for the dead.
- Harm, **Stroke, Ring, Press, Crack**: blow, sound, water's weight, break.
- Weight match, **weight** 1 to 5, a stroke that **taps**, rings **true** or **breaks**: a clapper must match its bell.
- Protections, **Weight, Muffle, Freeboard, Anneal**: mass, damping, height above water, toughened metal.
- Water, **bands** (dry, ankle, knee, deep), **flood, ebb, slack, spring**: tide marks on every pillar.
- Player's harm, **wound**, healed by **mending**: chalked where it landed.
- Buttons, **Trade** and **Heave**: the trade's tool; a find overboard.
- Gear, **castings**; goods carried, the **haul** of **finds**: all metal here was cast.
- Toll places, **landmarks** and the **bell-post**: where ringers stood at tolls.

## 2. The combat model

### 2.1 Kinds of harm

- **Stroke** asks weight. It lands at once, with its sigil's or attacker's weight, 1 to 5. On a body of its own weight it is **true**: full harm, and 50% again as Ring. On a lighter body it **breaks**: full harm, and 25% again as Crack. On a heavier body it **taps**: half harm.
- **Ring** asks muffling. It sounds in the body evenly over 3 s; starting or renewed, it passes 30% of the new Ring to up to 8 bodies of the same weight within 3 m, and passed Ring passes no further. On a muffled body it lands at once as Crack at half.
- **Press** asks depth. It lands at once, times the victim's band: dry 0.5, ankle 1, knee 1.5, deep 2. A body's band is the water at its feet, a band deeper for weight 5 and shallower for weight 1. Each Press on a body in water adds 1 Brine.
- **Crack** asks nothing. It takes life and greatest life together; each further quarter of its starting greatest life cracked drops a body a weight class (never keepers or the player). Bindings may convert other harm into it.

Defender order: weight match, Press band, Ring or its Crack, Anneal, harm-taken modifiers, wound.

### 2.2 Protections

- Against Stroke, **Weight**: class 1 below 50 Weight, 2 from 50, 3 from 100, 4 from 175, 5 from 275 (characters start at 50); lighter strokes tap. Cost, **Heft**: each class above 2 adds 0.08 s to reaching full pace, stopping and reversing, and class 5 sits a band deeper.
- Against Ring, **Muffle**: no Ring sounds in the character. Cost: it lands as Crack at half instead.
- Against Press, **Freeboard**: each point counts water a band shallower, two at most. Cost: each point carries the character 25% farther on currents and shoves.
- Against Crack, **Anneal**: takes its share off Crack, 60% at most. Cost: its main source, gun-metal, means little Tin.

A body is **Muffled** while its band is knee or deeper, while three other bodies touch it, or while wrapped in cloth.

### 2.3 The tide

Each toll turns the tide, odd tolls to flood and even to ebb, the level moving by 1, 2, 3, 3, 2, 1 twelfths of its range in steps of 12.5 s, slack 3 s either side of the toll. Every fourth toll is a double-range spring; the eighth, the keeper's, is the highest. Depth is level less ground height; current runs along each district's channels as fast as the level moves. Ring peaks on the ebb, Press on the flood and Brine on the turn, so light, heavy and water builds each lead for part of every 150 s.

### 2.4 Conditions

**Ringing.** From Ring: 12 even ticks over 3 s; new Ring adds to the rest, restarts the 3 s and spreads. Ends after 3 s, or on becoming Muffled, the rest landing as Crack at half.

**Muffled.** While a knee band, three touching bodies or a wrapping lasts: no Ringing, none passed on; Ring lands as Crack at half.

**Cracked.** Greatest life lowered and classes lost, so lighter blows begin to ring true. Enemies keep it; the player keeps it to the vigil's end, but standing within 6 m of the bell-post at a toll recasts a quarter.

**Swing.** Built only by alternation: a Stroke arriving more than 120° off the previous Stroke's direction adds 1, one within 60° removes 1, and it never fades. At 6 (keepers 12) the body goes over, thrown 4 m (keepers 1 m) along the last stroke, striking each body within 1 m of the path at its own weight for 10% of its greatest life; Swing returns to 0. The player is only shoved 2 m.

**Brined.** From Press in water, a stack each, 20 at most, inert while wet. On dry ground every stack lands as Crack worth 4% of greatest life (elites 2%, keepers 1%, the player 1.5%). Fresh water rinses all in 1 s.

**In Procession.** From following 5 s within 2.5 m of the player's path of the last 4 s: +15% speed, +25% harm taken, +25% experience, never crowd-muffled, countable up to 40. Ends on touching the player, a shove, or straying 3 m.

**Under Seal.** From wax. No condition expires, starts or ends, muffling included, so a sealed body rings on in a crowd or water and its Brine cannot land. Ends on one hit of 8% of greatest life or after 15 s; it works on the player too.

**Sounded.** From holding a weight 4 or 5 body or a keeper 4 to 6 m away for 2 s. For 6 s, renewed while held, every Stroke on it lands true whatever its weight.

### 2.5 How the player heals

Every Stroke, Ring or Press the player takes is chalked as a **wound** where it landed; Ring joins its stroke's wound, and wounds within 1 m join. Standing still within 1 m of a wound for its term (how long its blow was shown, at least 0.5 s) mends it. At most 12 lie open; a thirteenth hardens the oldest into Crack. Crack mends only at the bell-post (2.4); between vigils a character is whole.

### 2.6 The two buttons

**Trade** is the class's tool. **Heave** throws the heaviest find carried 5 m ahead as a Stroke of the thrower's weight, harm set by its worth (recharge 4 s); it drifts on the current until caught. Each find carried adds 3 Weight and takes 1.5% off pace.

### 2.7 Enemies and the player

Both sides share the rules except as 2.1 to 2.5 say and here: the dead's weights are fixed and the player's moves; the ordinary dead steer at the player's place of 1.2 s ago, elites and keepers at the player; only enemies are Sounded or In Procession, and they never mend.

### 2.8 What the screen shows

Size is weight; sigil icons show weight notches; the player's class by its life bar. A tap is a dull puff, a true stroke a bright ripple, a break grey chips; passed Ring is an arc to each body. Ringing pulses; Muffled shows a cloth band or knee water; Cracked a split, one size smaller; Swing a tick arc from 3; Brined white crust; Under Seal a red disc; Sounded two thin rings; a procession a thread along the path. Water is drawn as itself, with tide marks on pillars and the foot ring coloured by band; wounds are ticked chalk rings; one dial shows toll, tide and candle. The toll counts what comes: nine strokes for mostly weight 4 and 5 in the next 75 s, six for 2 and 3, three for 1; a quick stroke per elite; a low one if a gate opens.

### 2.9 The model in four sentences

Every sigil and every body has a weight from 1 to 5: a stroke of the body's own weight rings true, hurting it again over three seconds and passing some of that to like bodies nearby; a heavier stroke cracks it, taking greatest life that does not come back; a lighter one only taps for half. Water at the knee, a cloth wrapping or a tight crowd muffles a body, so it cracks instead of ringing. Press, the harm of water, grows with the depth at the victim's feet and leaves salt that cracks the body when it reaches dry ground, and the tide turns at every toll, so the best ground for each harm keeps moving. Your wounds stay where they landed: stand still on one for as long as its blow was shown before it fell, and it mends.

## 3. Attributes

- **Copper**: +5 greatest life, +1 Weight per point.
- **Tin**: +2% Stroke harm, +1% Ring length per point.
- **Rope**: +1% to how often sigils speak, +1% to their reach per point.
- **Metal**, read from Tin divided by Copper plus Tin: under 15% **gun-metal**, +40% Anneal; 15% to 25% **bell-metal**, the character's Ring spreads 50% farther and to 2 more bodies; over 25% **speculum**, +25% Stroke and +40% Crack taken.

Classes gain Copper/Tin/Rope each level: Ringer 3/1/1, Tuner 2/1/2, Leadsman 3/0/2, Warper 4/0/1, Sexton 4/1/0, Chandler 1/1/3.

**How things ask.** Everything equipped is cast in one metal or in any. A sigil of another metal than the character's speaks at 75%; a casting or binding of another metal works at half; a casting cast in a metal rolls a step truer than one cast in any. One point of Tin can carry the gauge across a mark and change half the kit.

## 4. Classes and callings

**Ringer**, a tower ringer who kept hold of the rope. *Thumb:* rocks back and forth in the crowd. *Sigil:* Tenor Stroke, weight 4, everything within 2.5 m every 3 s. *Rule:* each reversal over 120° is a pull, and every Stroke sigil at least half ready speaks at once (each at most once per 0.4 s); lifting the thumb 1 s sets the bell, doubling the next pull. Rocking past bodies strikes them from alternate sides, building Swing. *Trade, Set:* sets the bell while walking at half pace. *Callings:* **Stander**: while set, each body within 2 m adds its weight to a shown store (limit 15), and the next step releases every Stroke sigil at +10% per point; past 15 the stay breaks, the store is lost and no sigil speaks for 3 s. **Half-muffler**: pulls alternate handstroke and backstroke; a backstroke deals half, cannot ring, and wraps what it strikes for 2 s, so the next handstroke cracks it.

**Tuner**, a foundry tuner who cut bells to their note. *Thumb:* how far it is pushed. *Sigil:* Fork, a ring at the note's weight, from weight 5 (6 m, every 2.4 s) through 4 (5 m, 1.8 s), 3 (4 m, 1.3 s) and 2 (3 m, 0.9 s) to 1 (2 m, 0.6 s). *Rule:* a push under 20%, or standing, is weight 5, each further 20% one lighter, and every Stroke the Tuner makes takes that weight. *Trade, Hold:* keeps the note 5 s whatever the pace (recharge 12 s). *Callings:* **Gallery-walker**: a sigil cast within 2 m of a wall runs along it both ways 20 m at 10 m/s instead of spreading, striking once every body within 1.5 m of the wall. **Node-setter**: every 12 s a fork is planted, holding that note 12 s; on the line to the Node-setter (to 14 m) every 2 m a 1 m loud point strikes twice a second at that weight, and the still points halfway between take no Ring.

**Leadsman**, who sounds the flooded streets. *Thumb:* holds everything at 5 m, circling and stepping back. *Sigil:* Lead Line, a lead circling at 5 m every 1.5 s, striking only bodies 4.3 to 5.7 m away with a weight 3 Stroke and equal Press, times the bodies in the band over those inside (at least 1), up to 4 times. *Rule:* any body 1 s in the band is Sounded, whatever its weight. *Trade, Cast the lead:* the nearest weight 4 or 5 body within 10 m is Sounded at once (recharge 10 s). *Callings:* **Deep-caller**: standing still 1 s resets the band to the nearest body's distance, 2 to 8 m, until the next such stop. **Pilot**: under way within 1.5 m of the line from bell-post to last gate opened, the band widens 0.5 m and sigils reach 10% farther each second (to +3 m and +50%); stopping or straying loses both.

**Warper**, who moves hulks through the flood. *Thumb:* points; it never walks. *Sigil:* Kedge, each landing a weight 4 Stroke within 1.2 m. *Rule:* every 0.9 s a kedge flies 4.5 m where the thumb points (6 m in knee water) and the Warper is hauled to it in 0.35 s, unpushable, the taut line dealing Press to what it crosses; thumb lifted, it stands. *Trade, Belay:* makes fast, immovable, its last line fixed and dealing Press. *Callings:* **Snubber**: a line bent round a post swings the Warper round it, sweeping, to land beyond; a full turn snubs: 3 s immovable, shoving back whatever pushes. **Wrecker**: Belay instead lights the last kedge for 6 s; bodies within 12 m that see it walk straight at it, unable to turn, gaining 1 m/s a second, and what they run onto deals them a Stroke of their weight worth 5% of greatest life per m/s.

**Sexton**, who digs the parish graves. *Thumb:* lifts to dig and judges when to climb out. *Sigil:* Spadeful, every 1.2 s a weight 2 Stroke on the nearest body within 5 m, wrapping it 2 s. *Rule:* standing still digs a grave place a second, up to 8; inside, the Sexton is Muffled and reached only by shown strikes. A body walking in is laid, filling places equal to its weight (elites and keepers stop at the lip). Moving climbs out, 0.5 s a place, open to blows; a full grave closes. *Trade, Close:* shuts the grave, out in 0.3 s, the mound wrapping what stands on it 4 s. *Callings:* **Fossor**: each grave throws a 3 m spoil bank, opposite the Fossor's approach, that the dead cannot cross; they are turned round its ends toward the pit. **Sin-eater**: 0.5 s where a body fell, within 3 s, eats its sin: double experience and 1 Sin (+1% harm, -0.5% pace, no limit), shed only at a gate, a point to an Echo.

**Chandler**, the city's candle-maker. *Thumb:* runs loops out 12 m and back to the vat. *Sigil:* Broach, five weight 1 wicks lashing 3 m ahead every 1.5 s; three of one lash on a body put it Under Seal. *Rule:* each return within 2 m of the vat after going 12 m away adds a coat, up to 8, each +6% harm; a hit strips one and is halved. Coated, the Chandler is waxed: no Brine, but Press +25%. *Trade:* moves the vat to its feet (recharge 20 s). *Callings:* **Founder**: at 8 coats, a return with Trade held pours them into one Bronze for the vigil (+12% harm, +15 Weight, never stripped), and coats restart from none. **Tenebrist**: at each toll it darkens one of its sigils, vows or chimes for the vigil, and an heir of the same kind takes on all of it: a sigil's harm per second at that toll, a vow's or chime's effect (the vow's inches freed).

## 5. The world

**Pitch.** Tollbound is a cathedral-city on a tidal river whose great bells, rung at every turn of the tide, kept the drowned and the buried asleep. Now each bell swings at the turn without a sound, and every silent stroke wakes more dead. You are one of the last living ringers, sent from the Loft to keep vigil one district at a time: lay the dead, carry your finds out through the water-gates, and beat the keeper that comes at the eighth stroke, so the bell speaks again.

**Tone.** (1) Working people, working dead: no chosen ones, no villain, no gore. (2) Every effect has a buildable cause; nothing glows without a flame. (3) Silence is the danger: harm shows as stillness and rising water.

**Naming.** Plain English from bell-founding, river work, burial custom and candle-making; no invented syllables or Latin. Districts: "The" and a feature (The Founders' Sink). Bells: a short name (the Unpoured Bell). The dead: what they were or how they were buried (Bell-bearers, Salter). Sigils: a trade's verb or tool (Spadeful).

**The districts, in order.**

**The Founders' Sink.** Tapered-box furnaces, cylinder chimneys, cone slag, round pits; soot black, loam brown, ember orange, bronze, verdigris. Dry but for knee-deep channels. A Stroke in a pit is answered 1 s later by a 4 m Ring of the same harm on everyone; a pit answered over 3 times in 5 s cracks. Bell: the Unpoured Bell. Keeper: (1) a wax model whose eight faces record their harm, dripping wax that sets into pillars; (2) at half life, bronze: faces most harmed take double, untouched a fifth, and it turns them away, swinging a 4 m arc shown 1 s ahead.

**The Hanging Quarter.** Oak box frames, lead floors, striped cylinder sallies; oak brown, lead grey, wool red, chalk white, woad blue. Forty ropes tie bells overhead to circles 5 to 10 m away: whatever walks through a sally brings a weight 5 Stroke onto its circle 1 s later. Bell: the Dead Band. Its Conductor: six bells each tilt 0.6 s before a 40° wedge of Ring, in an order lamps show: (1) rounds; (2) at 70%, changes, each bell keeping its place or swapping with a neighbour; (3) at 30%, all six at once, except bells struck to nothing, which leave safe wedges.

**The Nave.** Two rows of cylinder pillars, box pews adrift; drowned green, slate, candle gold, lichen white, soot. The water also sloshes end to end every 24 s, one end deep while the other is ankle; the middle keeps its depth but runs fastest. Pews strike at weight 4; fonts rinse Brine. Bell: the Sacring Bell. Keeper of the Hearse, its 15 candles lighting 24° sectors: (1) a candle flares 1.2 s and its sector is swept and goes dark; touching the frame within 2 s relights it and wounds the keeper; (2) at 5 candles, two at once; (3) 5 s of dark and three claps, then triple harm taken for 6 s.

**The Strand.** Stepped-box stairs, cylinder posts, three tapered-box hulks; mud brown, tin-grey water, bone, weed green, buoy red. Each ebb bares 30 m of silt, slowing all but wearers of pattens and showing buried finds; each flood floats the hulks to ground elsewhere. Brine lands on the dry stairs. Bell: the Sunk Bell. Keeper, unharmable outside the bell: (1) every 20 s the bell's outline marks the player 1.5 s, then drops, shutting all within 5 m in with the keeper for 10 s on dry ground, a candle measuring the air (once out, all inside are harmed each second); (2) two candles, half the air.

**The Close.** Flagstones, crosses of two boxes on steps, a tapered-box west front; limestone, lead grey, verdigris, candle gold, window blue. Rings round the cathedral door at 30, 15 and 4 m cut the dead's harm by 20%, 40% and 70%, for 40 s of each per character per vigil. Bell and keeper: the Sleeper, carried by the dead: (1) tongueless, it charges along lines shown 1 s ahead, shedding four dead at each wall; (2) at 60%, re-tongued, it fills the arena with Ring every 6 s, the only shelter the lee 3 m behind a weight 5 body, or knee water where the Ring cracks at half; (3) at 25%, a 2 m strike per character level, then 8 s spent.

**The frame's fiction.** The **Loft**, the one tower above high water, holds the foundry and the **Carillon**, where founders recast each fallen ringer as a bell weighing the tolls it heard; the fallen never return (**one life**). Each toll a living ringer hears leaves **Echoes** in the stone at once. A district's bell swings silent at every turn of the tide (**the toll**), waking more dead and raising rewards; the eighth brings its keeper, whose fall gives the bell its voice. **Gates** are water-gates, open 25 s at the slack of every third toll and where an elite or keeper falls; standing in one rings down six strokes a second apart, each banking a sixth of the haul and silencing a sixth of the sigils; the sixth ends the vigil, and a ringer driven off sooner keeps what was banked. When all five bells speak, the districts join into one open city.

## 6. Progression

### The Plan: the shared tree

A street plan of Tollbound, about 700 nodes cut by the river, each class's door on its edge: **lanes** (attributes, small stats), **corners** (a stat with a tag or condition), **squares** (a rule). A point per level to 70, plus deeds: about 85. **One route:** points lay one unbranching walk from the door, each moving the head to a neighbour; stepping onto a held node costs a point and doubles it, once; points come back only from the head, for coin. **Beaten bounds:** closing a loop grants every node inside, up to 12, at half value, free. **Banks:** wet-bank nodes work only in water ankle-deep or more, dry-bank nodes only on dry ground, both at 160%; streets and bridges always at 100%. **Straight miles:** milestones gain 25% per held node beyond the fourth in their straight street, up to 10.

Planning is drawing with three strokes (straights for miles, loops for bounds, doubling back for doubles), weighing fenced ground against fence, in an order that matters, since only the head comes back; the bank asks the tide's question again.

### Castings: gear

A casting has a slot (tool, crown, yoke, gloves, girdle, pattens, two tokens), a Weight added to the wearer's, a metal or none, and up to five **lines**, each a clause from Section 2's rules (verb: stat and amount; object: tags; condition: when), such as I CRACK / THE HEAVY / IN DEEP WATER. A line rolls 0 to 6 steps sharp of true and works at 100% less 10% a step, or 200% exactly true; later tolls drop truer finds; one line in ten is mis-stamped, its condition reversed. No tiers: a casting is worth its distance from true and the Weight left to get there.

Foundry changes are one-way. **Lathe:** a cut moves a line a step toward true for 5% of the casting's Weight; past true it works at 50%; under 70% of cast Weight every line halves. **Chisel:** strikes a line's object or condition, widening it to everything, or always, at 60%. **Recast:** pours two or three castings of a slot into one, Weight their sum less 10%; the first poured gives the low lines, the last the high (two: 1 to 3, then 4 and 5; three: 1 and 2, 3, then 4 and 5); each toll the account hears before it is first worn moves its sharpest line a step toward true. Truing spends Weight, which is protection; recasting buys it back.

### Chimes, vows, callings, charts

**Chimes:** six stand in a visible row. At a level-up places 1 to 3 rise as shapes 4 m away, taken by walking into one within 8 s. A new chime enters at 6 and neighbours swap after each level-up, alternately 1-2, 3-4, 5-6 and 2-3, 4-5, so what is coming shows. **Vows:** the candle is 12 inches; each toll spends a free inch; a vow holds 2 to 5 inches back; with none free, vows go dark until a keeper drops 3 inches. **Callings and rites:** the calling, chosen at the first keeper beaten, is its rule and 8 points of nodes. A rite is a vigil keeping one custom from first toll to keeper (at most three finds; no wound hardened), worth 2 calling points. **Charts and deeds:** charts, dropped by keepers of voiced districts, carry up to three marks raising danger and Echoes; deeds grant classes, pits or castings.

### The Carillon: the permanent tree

**Echoes:** the Nth toll pays N times the number of different landmarks (each district marks six, the bell-post among them) the character has stood beside (within 4 m) at a toll this vigil, so every vigil pays, fatal or not. **The frame** branches from the tenor pit; Echoes open a pit beside an open one, each with a printed grant (flat or inc only). **The dead:** each dead or retired character becomes a bell weighing the tolls it heard, hung by weight in an open pit; it gives every later character a boon fixed by its class and calling, and names its killer, whose kind later characters strike 1% harder per 5 levels it had. **True ring:** each neighbouring pair whose weights differ by 10% to 30% adds a step to the ring bonus (1% harm, 2% Echoes), so a ringer who died young fills a place a veteran cannot.

### The Wake

Held for one dead standing character (before any death, an unknown ringer): the new character starts at level 1 in that class with its first three chimes taken and builds itself through five districts, a watch each; keep all five and the dead one's bell counts double.

## 7. Cost

Size; why it stays cheap with 600 enemies:
- Weight match, Crack, Press, Brine, Muffle, Swing (S): lookups per hit; touch counts for 1/30 of bodies a step; going over is one capsule query.
- Ring spread (M): one 3 m query per ring start; 8 receivers, who never query.
- Tide and ground (M): a level curve and a 1 m grid of height and channel; lookups, no fluid.
- Wake steering, In Procession (M): one ring buffer of the player's path and a grid of cells walked.
- Sounded, Under Seal, wounds, Heave, toll count, Echoes, gates, candle, chime row, Wake, rites, charts (S): heavy bodies only; player state; data; once a toll.
- Four classes' rules and the small callings (S each): player state; the band is one 6 m query a swing.
- Warper, Sexton's grave, Snubber, Wrecker, Fossor (M each): a query a haul or a step; nearby props; 60 lured bodies; 8 wall segments.
- Gallery-walker, Keeper of the Hearse (L each): walls as lines in the world; fifteen relit sectors and a phase by ear.
- District features (S), Strand hulks (M), four keepers (M each): fixed places; four moving boxes; existing boss machinery.
- The Plan, castings, the Carillon, walking into chimes (M each): allocation-time work and menus; offer objects.

**First version (the first vigil):** the eight medium items tide, Ring spread, wake steering, the Sexton's grave, castings, the Plan, the Carillon and the Keeper of the Unpoured Bell, plus every small item. **Held back:** the Warper and its callings, the Fossor, the Gallery-walker, walking into chimes (picked in a pause meanwhile), and each later district's features and keeper.
