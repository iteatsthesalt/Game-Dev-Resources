# Tollbound spine: the game in the hand

Priority: one thumb in a crowd. Every rule is something the feet do, shown as a shape on the ground. Four survivors lock together and carry the rest: harm sorted by posture (thumbs-01), the dead walking your own path (thumbs-55), lost life owed to the place it was lost (thumbs-02, changed form) and crossing your own path (thumbs-07 changed form, thumbs-27). Classes, gear and both trees read the same postures: still, moving, near, timed.

## 2. The combat model

**Kinds of harm.** Every voice and every attack deals one kind.
- **Blow**: one hit: a strike, a swing, a thrown thing. An enemy Blow shows as its striking shape rising for its wind-up, 0.3 to 0.8 s.
- **Drag**: harm counted 4 times a second, without warning: a pressing crowd, riders, bad ground, a lingering voice.
- **Knell**: a heavy strike drawn on the ground as a shape that fills until it lands, 0.8 to 3 s. It ignores every modifier of its target except Wrong-footed.

**Protections: what the feet are doing.**
- **Brace**, against Blows: stand still 0.4 s and Blows taken are halved until the first step.
- **Stride**, against Drag: move at 70% of top speed or more and Drag taken is cut by 60%.
- **Lee**, against Knells: a spreading Knell, drawn outward from a source, spares a body that has a prop or a large body (elite, keeper, heavy dead, burgess) on the line to the source within 3 m of it. A falling Knell, a circle darkening underfoot, has no lee: step out.

Standing in a crowd braces you against its Blows and gives you to its Drag; no posture covers all three.

**Wrong-footed.** Any body, living or dead, whose heading changed more than 90° within the last 0.5 s takes 50% more from all harm.

**How the dead move.** The common dead steer at the place you stood 1.2 s ago. Walking draws them into a train on your exact path; they turn where you turned, 1.2 s later, and are Wrong-footed at every corner you cut. Stop, and they reach you in 1.2 s.

**Conditions.**
1. **Wrong-footed.** As above; ends 0.5 s after the turn (keepers 0.25 s).
2. **In Procession.** Applied: a dead stays within 2.5 m of your wake for 5 s. Does: +15% speed, +30% harm taken, +25% experience. Ends: it touches you, is shoved, or strays 3 m off the wake. Interacts: the count (cap 40) is a stat voices and knots can read.
3. **Moored.** Applied: when your path crosses your wake of the last 5 s, a stake is driven at the crossing and the dead within 2.5 m, up to 12, are tied to it in the order they followed you. Does: each moves only within 2.5 m of the stake; felling or shoving one jerks the next 1 m inward and Wrong-foots it. Ends: after 4 s or below 2 bodies; one stake per 3 s, no body twice in 6 s. Interacts: the moored stand, so they Brace and take Drag in full.
4. **Sounded.** Applied: keep a large dead 4 to 6 m away for 2 s. Does, for 6 s: every harm range against it rolls its top value, and its attacks show 0.3 s earlier. Ends: 0.5 s outside the band.
5. **Coiled.** Applied: each full sunwise circuit you walk round a large dead within 6 m adds a Coil (max 3). Does: its attacks come 12% slower per Coil; one widdershins circuit spends them all, each a Blow of 150% of your strongest voice hit. Ends: 10 s after the last circuit. Interacts: circling at 4 to 6 m also Sounds it.
6. **Mourning.** Applied: when an elite or keeper dies, to every common dead within 8 m. Does: 6 s full (black, stands facing the body, will not strike), then 6 s half (grey, half speed, half the strikes). Ends: on time; harm during full mourning ends it and the body strikes 30% faster for 6 s. Interacts: mourners stand, so Blows do half and Drag wakes them; walk away and they sit out 12 s.
7. **Clung.** Applied: hangers-on, small dead, leap onto you from 1.5 m. Does: per rider (max 5), 8% slower and one Drag a second; standing still lets another within 3 m climb on every 2 s. Ends: never by time; pass within 0.8 m of a large dead at Stride speed and every rider moves onto it. Interacts: riders pull you below Stride, so their Drag lands in full.
8. **Coined.** Applied: by voices, knots and favours that say so, for 5 s. Does: it walks straight on, turning only at props, and strikes whatever it walks into, the dead included. Ends: on time, or a Knell within 4 m; it drops two coins, four if laid while coined. Interacts: it leaves the wake, so coin the head of a train and step aside.

**Healing: wounds owed to a place.** Harm that lands on you opens a wound on the ground where you stood, holding the life it took; harm within 2 m of an open wound joins the newest one, and at most 10 are open. A wound's depth is the warning its harm was shown for: Drag 0, a Blow its wind-up, a Knell its fill; a joined wound keeps the deepest. Stand inside a wound (1 m) and after 0.4 s it gives its life back evenly over 1 s + 2 × depth; stepping off keeps what came back. A wound open for 60 s scars: what is left comes off greatest life until the vigil ends, and its rim greys as that nears. Nothing else heals unless a voice, knot, favour or vow says so; scars are left at the lychgate. The dead neither wound nor mend.

**Heed and vows.** An attack locks its aim the moment it is first shown. Heed, 100 per character, that is not held by vows shows every Knell earlier, up to 1 s at 100 free Heed; Ear, the Register, Sounded and lit lamps add to that, never past 1 s in all. A vow holds Heed for a promise the thumb keeps or breaks; broken, it pays nothing until the next toll. Examples: Stability (30: stay within 12 m of where you stood at the last toll; +25% harm); the Unbroken Round (35: touch the bell-post between every two tolls; +5% harm and +2% speed per kept period, to 8; one miss empties it); Alms (25: leave each toll period's first find lying as a cairn worth +5% experience while unclaimed; taking it back breaks the vow); Telling the Bees (20: a hive fetches pickups within 10 m, but within 10 s of an elite falling you must stand still 1 s, or it leaves until the next toll with its load).

**The two extra buttons.** Button one is the class verb, shown only where one exists: the Toll-keeper's post and the Backward-ringer's alarm. Button two, every class, is **Cast Away**: your newest find is thrown 6 m ahead, the common dead within 10 m crowd round it for 5 s, and it is destroyed.

**The dead and you.** Both obey every rule above: hit the running, grind the standing. Only you wound and mend; you can carry only Wrong-footed and Clung; keepers cannot be Moored, Coined or put in mourning; your attributes sharpen your postures, and theirs never change.

**What the screen shows.**
- Under every body its posture: a closed ring when braced, a streak when striding, a white flash when wrong-footed. The crowd's marks say which voices will bite.
- Spreading Knells as filling shapes with their lee cut out; falling Knells as darkening circles.
- Your wake as a faint line; a stake and taut lines while bodies are moored.
- Wounds as red discs, darker when deeper, a ring closing while you mend, a rim greying toward a scar.
- Conditions as shape and colour, never icons: bowed heads, black and grey, two bright discs, coils at the base, two thin rings, boxes on the figure.
- Heed as a thin bar under life; the toll as a ring closing round the screen edge in its last 3 s.

**The model in four sentences.**
1. There are three kinds of harm: a Blow is one hit, Drag keeps biting while you are in a crowd or on bad ground, and a Knell is a great strike drawn on the ground before it lands.
2. Stand still to brace against Blows, run hard to shake off Drag, and put a pillar or a big body between you and a Knell; the dead are protected the same way, so hit the running and grind the standing.
3. Anyone who has just turned sharply is Wrong-footed and takes half as much again, and the dead walk the path you walked, so they turn wherever you turned.
4. The life you lose stays on the ground where you lost it: stand on it to take it back before it scars.

## 3. Attributes

One for each thing the thumb does: stop, go, close in, wait for the moment.
- **Knee**: +2 greatest life; +1% to effects tagged Still. Every 10: Brace takes hold 0.03 s sooner (least 0.1 s) and cuts Blows 2 points more (at most 70%).
- **Heel**: +0.4% top speed; +1% to effects tagged Moving. Every 10: Stride holds at 2 points less of top speed (least 50%).
- **Hand**: +1% to effects on dead within 3 m; +1% pickup reach. Every 10: wounds mend from 0.1 m farther (at most 2 m).
- **Ear**: +1% to effects tagged Timed (voices that speak on the quarters, the toll or an appointment). Every 10: Knells show 0.05 s sooner, within the 1 s limit.

Every voice and item names one attribute and a number wanted. Nothing is locked: below the number, all it gives works at your value divided by the number. A voice wants the attribute of its tag, 10 per tier (1 to 5); an item wants that of the posture that wrote most of its lines, 4 per line. A level gives 2 points to the class's first attribute and 1 to its second; tree path nodes give points of the player's choice.

## 4. Classes and methods

**Ringer** (Hand, Knee). A tower ringer who kept hold of the rope when the bells fell dumb. The thumb rocks back and forth inside the crowd.
- Starts with Tenor Stroke: a Blow ring 2.5 m round the Ringer every 3 s.
- Rule, the pull: a reversal of more than 120° within 0.3 s makes the voice in the first place speak at once (at most once per 0.4 s) and does not Wrong-foot the Ringer. Standing still 1 s sets the bell: the next pull speaks double. The train turns where the Ringer turned and is Wrong-footed into the next pull.
- Stander: while set, each dead within 2 m leans on the stay with its weight (common 1, large 3, elite 5), a bar rising toward 12. The next pull makes every voice speak once in order, 0.1 s apart, each +8% per weight leaning; past 12 the stay breaks and nothing speaks for 3 s.
- Backward-ringer: button one rings backward: a mark is set where the Ringer stands, every common dead in the district walks to it instead of the wake, and each second backward brings the next toll 1 s nearer. Pressed again, the gathered crowd turns for the Ringer at once.

**Verger** (Heel, Hand). Carried the verge that cleared the road before a procession. The thumb drives straight through the thickest crowd.
- Starts with the Verge: every 0.9 s it raps the two dead nearest the wedge's edges (Blow).
- Rule, the wedge: moving at half top speed or more and not Wrong-footed, the Verger opens a wedge 3 m long and 120° wide ahead; the dead in it are shouldered 1.5 m aside and cannot strike for 0.5 s. Behind and beside are bare. The parted dead turn about for the wake and are Wrong-footed behind him.
- Beadle: the parted dead stand 5 s in two still files facing inward along the lane just walked, unable to strike; if the Verger turns back into the lane, both files strike inward at once. Standing, the files Brace: the lane is laid for Drag.
- Marshal: while the Verger stands still, every dead in procession halts, up to 4 s, not again for 6 s; the rest keep coming. He chooses when his followers are runners and when standers.

**Toll-keeper** (Hand, Heel). Kept the bar at bridge and gate. The thumb circles a post, sweeping the following train like a clock hand.
- Starts with Pike: a 4 m thrust at the nearest dead every 1.5 s (Blow).
- Rule, the bar: button one plants the toll-post where the keeper stands. Within 7 m of it a bar joins post and keeper, and every dead it passes over pays toll: a Blow that doubles each time the same body pays again within 10 s, at most 8 times. Farther away the bar lifts.
- Parish Clerk: at each toll the kind of dead laid most since the last toll is struck off the roll and none of it rise in the next interval, while every other kind rises 15% more. The roll fills on screen as the player steers toward the kind to be rid of.
- Alderman: a dead that pays three times within 10 s and still stands becomes a burgess: it stops, cannot harm or be harmed, blocks like stone, gives lee, and adds 1 coin to your finds at each toll it stands. The bar becomes a choice between killing and building paying walls.

**Tuner** (Ear, Heel). A foundry tuner who knows every bell by its note. The thumb sets the note by how far it pushes.
- Starts with Fork: a Blow ring, 6 m every 2.4 s at the lowest note, 2 m every 0.6 s at the highest.
- Rule, pace is pitch: push depth sets the note of every voice in five steps. Under 40% push (tenor) voices wait 2.5 times as long, reach twice as far and strike 2.2 times as hard; over 85% (treble) they wait 0.4 as long, reach half as far and strike at 0.55; the steps between scale evenly. Standing still holds the last note. Treble is a run and Strides; tenor has neither Brace nor Stride, so the heavy note is paid for in exposure.
- Gallery-walker: a voice cast within 2 m of a wall does not spread; it runs both ways along the wall for 20 m at 10 m/s, striking once every body within 1.5 m of it.
- Deep-caller: standing still 1 s sets every voice's reach to the distance of the nearest dead (2 to 8 m), drawn as a chalk ring, until the next such stop; notes then change only pace and weight.

**Lamplighter** (Ear, Heel). Walked the round of the city's lamps at dusk. The thumb plans a circuit of fixed posts and keeps it against the clock.
- Starts with Taper: every 2 s each lit lamp throws a spark (Blow) at the dead nearest it in its light.
- Rule, the round: six lamp-posts stand in every district. Passing within 2 m lights one for 45 s; every toll puts all out. Each lit lamp makes your voices speak 10% faster, and Knells inside its 5 m light show 0.4 s sooner.
- Snuffer: passing a lit lamp again snuffs it: a Knell fills its light in 0.5 s, its harm the seconds burned times a base, and the dead it strikes walk on their last heading for 3 s. Every post asks: keep the light or cash its age.
- Remembrancer: each lamp keeps up to 6 casts your voices made in its light on your last visit and repeats them at half harm while it burns; the round becomes a score of which voice spoke where.

**Organ-blower** (Knee, Heel). Pumped the cathedral organ's bellows in a dark loft. The thumb runs long, then stands short.
- Starts with Pipe: a Blow cone 6 m long and 40° wide toward where it last walked.
- Rule, wind: voices never speak while it moves. Walking fills the wind-chest 1 unit per metre, to 30, shown as a weight on a string above the figure; standing still lets the wind out, voices speaking at three times their rate and spending 10 units a second until it is empty. It runs through Drag in Stride and stands to fire in Brace.
- Treader: standing, the crowd's press is wind: every 5 dead within 3 m add 1 unit each half second. On the first step the wind left runs out through the spot as a race 2 m wide and up to 10 m long, away from the densest side, dealing Drag by the wind it carries.
- Organist: standing, each voice holds its note until 5 dead stand inside what it would strike, or 4 s pass; each full second held adds 20% harm and spends no wind. The crowd arrives 1.2 s after the stop, so the Organist stops where it will be thickest.
