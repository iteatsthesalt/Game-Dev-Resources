# Cinderdeep lives in one place

Cinderdeep 3D and the 8-Way Sprite Rig (with its 3D models) are combined into one game in
[iteatsthesalt/CinderDeep](https://github.com/iteatsthesalt/CinderDeep), branch `main`:

- the game: `projects/cinderdeep-3d/`
- the sprite rig and the models: `projects/sprite-rig/`

Work there. Do not restore, copy or develop Cinderdeep 3D or the sprite rig in this repository.
`Games/Cinderdeep/` is the original 2D game the 3D game was made from; it is kept as a reference and is not developed.
